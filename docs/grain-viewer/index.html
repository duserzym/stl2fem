    About this viewer

    Original is the per-particle STL published by Nikolaisen et al. (2022) (FIB-SEM slice-and-view, 25 nm slices; segmented in FIJI, meshed in ParaView, separated in Meshmixer, solidified with Iso2Mesh). Vertices are welded; geometry is otherwise untouched.

    Processed is the outer boundary of the tetrahedral Gmsh 2.2 mesh used for MERRILL micromagnetic modeling, produced by stl2fem and selected per grain by the downstream mesh inventories (PLAG: 245-grain surface-fill inventory; OPX: 82-grain 9 nm v2 inventory). Edge, tetrahedron and volume statistics use the full volume mesh.

    Both surfaces are shown in nanometres in the FIB sample frame, translated so that the raw-STL bounding-box centre is the origin; they are therefore registered and directly comparable. The camera is orthographic, so the scale bar is exact anywhere in the view.

    Flags (processed mesh, ⚑ in the list): no processed mesh; voxel fallback or refused remesh; processed volume differs from the published volume by more than , or from a closed raw STL by more than ; more than one connected piece; mesh hash differs from its inventory.

    Notes (published input): most PLAG STLs are open, non-manifold and not consistently orientable. Their enclosed volume is undefined, so for those grains the processed mesh is compared with the published volume only, and the raw surface is drawn flat-shaded.

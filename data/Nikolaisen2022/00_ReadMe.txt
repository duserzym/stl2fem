// Description of dataset: 
The dataset contains a collection of 302 binary and ASCII Stereolithographic (STL) meshes of magnetite hosted by orthopyroxene and plagioclase. 
These meshes can be used to replicate the results (Mrs/Ms, Hc, remanence vectors and susceptibility of remanence) presented in "OPX_B16.csv", "Plag_B16.csv"
and the article: "Hysteresis parameters and magnetic anisotropy of silicate-hosted magnetite inclusions". 
"OPX_stl_B16.csv" and "Plag_stl_B16.csv" show the geometric representations for each individual grains that were used in the calculations.
  
--------------------------
METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION
--------------------------

// Description of sources and methods used for collection/generation of data:
The volumes representing Plag_Meshes.stl and OPX_Meshes.stl are extracted through the method of "Slice and View" by FIB-SEM nanotomography at the NanoLab, NTNU. This is the consept
of milling silicates by on an electron microscope by a Focused Ion Beam (FIB) into slices 25nm thick that are imaged for each slice with an electron beam. 
Each slice is then assembled in order, aligned and edited/segmented in FIJI (ImageJ). The segmented magnetite particles are generated to meshes in Paraview,
separated into individual particles in Meshmixer and solidified in MatLab by Iso2Mesh. 

// Methods for processing the data:
The resulted magnetic hysteresis properties is then calculated in MERRILL for each particle with 20 equidistributed external fields, at strength of 350 to -350 mT.

- "OPX_stl_B16.csv" 
- "Plag_stl_B16.csv" 
(lists the volume in cubic micrometer, equlivalent sphere diameter (EVSD) in micrometer, Center of gravity (COG), Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Bearing and plunge of the particles long axis and the Spheroidness (Ob_Sph_Pro) for each particle in the orthopyroxene and plagioclase.
The values in both "OPX_stl_B16.csv" and "Plag_stl_B16.csv" represents particles with minimal smothing and editing. These particles can be extracted from the "OPX_Meshes.stl" and "Plag_Meshes.stl")

---------------------------------------

- "OPX_B16.csv" 
- "Plag_B16.csv"
(lists the micromagnetic hysteresis results, describing the domaine structure from minimizing a random seed in 0 field, Hc, Mr/Ms and Hcr (Not for OPX) for the orthopyroxene and plagioclase particles that were modelled.)

---------------------------------------

- "OPX_Meshes.stl"
- "Plag_Meshes.stl"
(unfiltered binary STL (stereolithography) files that represent the meshes from the raw milled volume within both the pyroxene and plagioclase. These can be further subdivided into individual particles and then used for micromagnetic modelling.)

---------------------------------------

- "OPX_Pat_B16.csv"
- "Plag_Pat_B16.csv"
(Patran files for micromagnetic modelling in MERRILL. Average edge length for tetrahedrons is calculated and listed with the volume, number of nodes and tetrahedrons for each individual file in the OPX_Pat_B16.csv/Plag_Pat_B16.csv)
